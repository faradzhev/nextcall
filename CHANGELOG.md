# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Menu bar item with a popover of today's and tomorrow's meetings (1 to 7 days), read from the calendars on your Mac.
- One-click join for 21 providers, with link detection in the event URL, location and notes, including HTML and
  wrapped links (Outlook SafeLinks, Google redirects, Proofpoint).
- Menu bar countdown, accent colour in the last five minutes, and a "now" state.
- Reminders with Join and Snooze, and an optional full-screen alert that dims the screen.
- Global hotkeys, per-provider open mode (app or browser) and browser choice.
- Event filtering for personal blocks, plus a context menu to copy the link, open in Calendar, email attendees,
  hide an event or ignore its title.
- First-launch welcome flow, English and Ukrainian, VoiceOver and keyboard support.
